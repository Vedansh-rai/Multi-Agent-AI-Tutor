# Common Mistakes in JEE Mathematics

## Algebra Mistakes
1. Forgetting ± in square root: √(x²) = |x|, NOT x
2. Canceling variables without checking if they can be zero
3. Distributing exponents incorrectly: (a+b)² ≠ a² + b²
4. Wrong sign in completing the square
5. Forgetting that log is only defined for positive arguments
6. Confusing AM-GM direction: AM ≥ GM, not AM ≤ GM

## Calculus Mistakes
1. Forgetting the chain rule in composite functions
2. Not adding constant of integration C in indefinite integrals
3. Confusing d/dx[sin⁻¹(x)] with 1/sin(x)
4. Wrong limits after substitution in definite integrals
5. Forgetting to check continuity before applying L'Hôpital
6. Not verifying the 0/0 or ∞/∞ form before L'Hôpital
7. Differentiating definite integral without Leibniz rule when limits depend on x

## Probability Mistakes
1. Not checking independence before multiplying probabilities
2. Confusing P(A|B) with P(B|A)
3. Forgetting to subtract intersection in P(A ∪ B)
4. Using permutation when combination is needed (order matters vs. not)
5. Not considering complementary counting when direct counting is complex

## Linear Algebra Mistakes
1. Assuming all matrices are invertible
2. Matrix multiplication is NOT commutative: AB ≠ BA in general
3. det(A + B) ≠ det(A) + det(B)
4. Rank of product: rank(AB) ≤ min(rank(A), rank(B))
5. Eigenvalues of A + B ≠ eigenvalues of A + eigenvalues of B

## General Problem-Solving Mistakes
1. Not reading the question fully — missing conditions
2. Not checking if the answer satisfies original constraints
3. Ignoring domain restrictions (division by zero, log domain)
4. Not verifying edge cases (n=0, n=1)
5. Rounding intermediate steps — keep exact until final answer
